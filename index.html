‸ About

Editorial judgment, powered by modern tools.

I'm a freelance writer working at the intersection of language and technology. My process starts with AI-assisted drafting — it's fast, it's tireless, and it's excellent at getting a rough shape onto the page. But nothing goes to a client unedited. Every draft is rewritten, fact-checked, and shaped by hand until it sounds like a person who actually understands the business wrote it.

I think the future of good writing isn't "AI vs. human" — it's AI for speed, human for judgment. Clients get faster turnaround without losing the nuance, accuracy, and voice that make copy actually work.

Writing philosophy

  • Clarity beats cleverness. If a reader has to work to understand a sentence, rewrite it.
  • Every claim gets checked. AI drafts confidently — accuracy is the human's job.
  • Voice is not optional. Generic copy is invisible copy.
  • Structure serves the reader, not the writer's ego.

Core skills

Copywriting SEO Fundamentals Brand Voice Research & Fact-Checking Editing & Proofreading Content Strategy Prompt Engineering Storytelling

Values

  • Honesty — I disclose the AI-assisted process; nothing is hidden.
  • Accuracy — Unverified claims don't ship.
  • Craft — Every draft is edited until it earns the send.

‸ Process

From rough draft to final copy.

01

Research

Understand the business, the audience, and the competitive landscape before a single word is written.

02

Planning

Outline structure, key messages, and tone — the blueprint the draft will follow.

03

AI Draft

Generate a fast first pass to get raw material and structure onto the page.

04

Human Editing

Rewrite for voice, tighten sentences, cut filler, and inject the specifics AI can't know.

05

Fact-Checking

Verify every claim, number, and reference against a real source before it ships.

06

Final Polish

A last read for rhythm, clarity, and formatting — ready to publish.
