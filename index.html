06 The studio

Human taste in an
automated world.

I'm a freelance writer working at the intersection of language and technology.

AI is excellent at getting a rough shape onto the page. But nothing ships unedited. Every draft is rewritten, fact-checked, and shaped by hand until it sounds like a person who actually understands the business wrote it.

The future of good writing isn't AI versus human. It is AI for speed, human for judgment.
